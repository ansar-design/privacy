আমার বর্ণমালা – অক্ষর শিখি (Amar Bornomala) — Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026 (first version 22 October 2026) · App ID com.amirahsworld.bornomala · Developer: Tosa Creations Ltd (app brand: Amirah's World; Syed Ansar Ullah), Dhaka, Bangladesh

“আমার বর্ণমালা – অক্ষর শিখি (Amar Bornomala)” is a learning app for young children. It teaches Bangla and English letters and numbers with pictures, recorded voices, finger tracing, games, rhymes, stories and colouring pages. It has two modes: General and Islamic (Arabic letters, kalimas, duas, salah steps, short surahs). A child can switch between the two modes with the mode button; a parent can turn this off in the parents' settings.

What we collect

The app itself sends nothing to us or to anyone (the only exceptions are a picture or a feedback email that a parent chooses to send with the phone's own apps, described below): the Android app uses the internet only for the optional AI handwriting check described below, and neither the Android nor the iPhone/iPad app sends usage or personal data to our servers. It collects no email, photo, location, contacts or account details. There is no sign-up and no login. A parent may optionally type the child's first name and choose an age band in Settings; these stay on the device only.

Feedback by email (only if a parent chooses)

Behind the parents-only lock there is a feedback button. It opens the phone's own email app with a draft; nothing is sent unless the parent presses send. If you send it, we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to reply and to improve the app. We do not add you to any list. We keep feedback emails only as long as needed to answer and fix the problem, and delete them within 12 months; ask us at the address below and we will delete them sooner.

What stays on the device

Stars earned, letters learned, voice settings, the pictures the child colours and the child's first name (only if a parent types it in Settings) are saved on the device itself so the app remembers them. The app never sends them anywhere; only a coloured picture that a parent chooses to share (see below) leaves the device, as a copy. Deleting the app deletes them.

No ads, no tracking, no sharing

The app shows no advertising, contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs (it is built with the open-source Capacitor app framework; the Android app also includes Google ML Kit Digital Ink for the optional handwriting check below), has no in-app purchases, no social media links and no chat. The app itself shares no data with anyone; the developer receives nothing unless a parent emails us.

Coloured pictures stay on the device. Behind a parents-only lock, a parent may choose to save them to the phone or share them using the phone's own share menu; the parent picks where they go, and the app itself never uploads them. A picture shared this way is a copy outside the app; it goes only to the person or app the parent chose and is not deleted when the app is removed.

Internet, sound, camera and microphone

All voices are audio files stored inside the app, so the app works without internet. Many of them were made with AI text-to-speech (Google Gemini) while we built the app and were checked before release; nothing is sent to any AI service while your child uses the app. If a recording is missing, the device's own text-to-speech reads the word; the app asks for an on-device voice, but the phone's speech engine is outside our control. The app does not use the camera or microphone.

Optional AI handwriting check (Android)

It is off until a parent switches it on behind the parents-only lock. When switched on, the app downloads Google ML Kit's handwriting files for Bangla and English once (about 20-40 MB); this is the only reason the Android app has the internet permission. The check then runs on the phone itself: the letters your child writes are never sent anywhere and are not stored. While the feature is in use, Google ML Kit may send Google limited diagnostic information (such as app and device details, performance and error data) to keep the service working; it does not send the child's writing. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure. Switching the check off deletes the downloaded files. The check is kind: it can only add praise or a bonus star, never fail a child, and AI can be wrong.

Our story

This app began with my little daughter, Amirah. I could not find an app I truly liked for teaching her the alphabet, so I started building a small one for her with the help of AI. Little by little, as I kept improving it for her, it grew bigger and more beautiful. Then I felt that every child deserves an app like this, so I polished it with care and opened it to everyone.

If this app helps your child even a little, all my effort is worth it. Please keep my daughter in your prayers.
— Syed Ansar Ullah, Amirah's father

Children

The app is made for children up to 8 years old (mainly about 2 to 6), with a parent nearby. Because no personal information is collected, none is collected, used or disclosed about children.
