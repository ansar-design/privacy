আমার বর্ণমালা – অক্ষর শিখি (Amar Bornomala) — Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026 (first version 22 October 2026) · App ID com.amirahsworld.bornomala · Developer: Tosa Creations Ltd (app brand: Amirah's World; Syed Ansar Ullah), Dhaka, Bangladesh

“আমার বর্ণমালা – অক্ষর শিখি (Amar Bornomala)” is a learning app for young children. It teaches Bangla and English letters and numbers with pictures, recorded voices, finger tracing, games, rhymes, stories and colouring pages. It has two modes: General and Islamic (Arabic letters, kalimas, duas, salah steps, short surahs). A child can switch between the two modes with the mode button; a parent can turn this off in the parents' settings.

What we collect

The app itself sends nothing to us or to anyone, except a feedback message that a parent chooses to write in the parents-only feedback box (described below), or a picture or email a parent chooses to send with the phone's own apps. The app uses the internet only for that feedback box and for the store's own purchase check (buy or restore the one-time unlock); neither the Android nor the iPhone/iPad app sends usage or personal data to our servers. It collects no email, photo, location, contacts or account details. There is no sign-up and no login. A parent may optionally type the child's first name and choose an age band in Settings; these stay on the device only.

Feedback (only if a parent chooses)

Behind the parents-only lock (a grown-up sum) there is a feedback box. If a parent writes a message there and taps Send, we receive the message, the app version and the phone model and system version (for example "Android 14; SM-A145F"); there is no name, contact, account or device ID field, and no location. If there is no internet, the message waits on the phone and is sent later. It is stored on our own server (Cloudflare, used as our processor) and used only to fix and improve the app; we never sell or share it, and we delete it within 12 months. Parents can also email us instead: then we receive your email address and your message, and we use them only to reply and to improve the app. We do not add you to any list. We keep feedback emails only as long as needed to answer and fix the problem, and delete them within 12 months; ask us at the address below and we will delete them sooner.

What stays on the device

Stars earned, letters learned, voice settings, the pictures the child colours and the child's first name (only if a parent types it in Settings) are saved on the device itself so the app remembers them. The app never sends them anywhere; only a coloured picture that a parent chooses to share (see below) leaves the device, as a copy. Deleting the app deletes them. On Android, the phone's own backup (if the family has it switched on) may copy these stars, progress, settings and pictures to the family's own Google account backup, so a new phone keeps them; this is Android's service, and we never receive it.

No ads, no tracking, no sharing

The app shows no advertising, contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs (it is built with the open-source Capacitor app framework, plus the store's own purchase library from Google Play or Apple), has no social media links and no chat. Outside Bangladesh, after a 7-day free trial, a parent can unlock the full app once (one-time purchase, no subscription) behind the parents-only lock. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play or Apple; we never see card or payment details and receive no personal data from it. To decide whether the app is free, the app reads the store country and the phone's time zone on the phone only; this is never sent to us. The app itself shares no data with anyone; the developer receives nothing unless a parent sends feedback.

Coloured pictures stay on the device. Behind a parents-only lock, a parent may choose to save them to the phone or share them using the phone's own share menu; the parent picks where they go, and the app itself never uploads them. A picture shared this way is a copy outside the app; it goes only to the person or app the parent chose and is not deleted when the app is removed.

Internet, sound, camera and microphone

The app works fully offline; internet is used only if a parent sends feedback or buys/restores the one-time unlock through the store. All voices are audio files stored inside the app. Many of them were made with AI text-to-speech (Google Gemini) while we built the app and were checked before release; nothing is sent to any AI service while your child uses the app. If a recording is missing, the device's own text-to-speech reads the word; the app asks for an on-device voice, but the phone's speech engine is outside our control. The app does not use the camera or microphone.

Our story

This app began with my little daughter, Amirah. I could not find an app I truly liked for teaching her the alphabet, so I started building a small one for her with the help of AI. Little by little, as I kept improving it for her, it grew bigger and more beautiful. Then I felt that every child deserves an app like this, so I polished it with care and opened it to everyone.

If this app helps your child even a little, all my effort is worth it. Please keep my daughter in your prayers.
— Syed Ansar Ullah, Amirah's father

মতামত (শুধু বাবা-মা পাঠালে): বাবা-মায়ের তালার (বড়দের অঙ্ক) পেছনে একটি মতামতের বাক্স আছে। বাবা-মা লিখে "পাঠান" চাপলে আমরা পাই শুধু সেই লেখা, অ্যাপের ভার্সন আর ফোনের মডেল ও সিস্টেম ভার্সন। কোনো নাম, ফোন নম্বর, অ্যাকাউন্ট, ডিভাইস আইডি বা লোকেশন/দেশ রাখা হয় না। ইন্টারনেট না থাকলে লেখাটি ফোনে অপেক্ষা করে, পরে যায়। এটি আমাদের নিজের সার্ভারে (Cloudflare) থাকে, শুধু অ্যাপ ঠিক ও ভালো করতে ব্যবহার হয়, কারও কাছে বিক্রি বা শেয়ার করা হয় না, আর ১২ মাসের মধ্যে মুছে ফেলা হয়। ইমেইলে মতামত পাঠালে আপনার ইমেইল ঠিকানা শুধু উত্তর দিতে ব্যবহার করি, ১২ মাসের মধ্যে মুছে ফেলি; বললে আগেই মুছে দেব।
