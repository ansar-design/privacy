আমার বর্ণমালা · My First Letters — Privacy Policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · App ID com.amirahsworld.bornomala · Developer: Amirah's World (Syed Ansar Ullah), Dhaka, Bangladesh

“আমার বর্ণমালা · My First Letters” is a learning app for young children. It teaches Bangla and English letters and numbers with pictures, recorded voices, finger tracing and a picture game.

What we collect

Nothing is sent to us or to anyone. The app collects no email, photo, location, contacts or account details. There is no sign-up and no login. A parent may optionally type the child's first name and choose an age band in Settings; these stay on the device only.

What stays on the device

Stars earned, letters learned, voice settings and the child's first name (only if a parent types it in Settings) are saved on the device itself so the app remembers them. They never leave the device. Deleting the app deletes them.

No ads, no tracking, no sharing

The app shows no advertising, contains no analytics or tracking tools, has no in-app purchases, no social media links and no chat. It shares no data with anyone.

Internet, sound, camera and microphone

All voices are recorded audio stored inside the app, so the app works without internet. If a recording is missing, the device's own text-to-speech reads the word; the app asks for an on-device voice, but the phone's speech engine is outside our control. The app does not use the camera or microphone.

Children

The app is made for children aged 2 to 6, with a parent nearby. Because no personal information is collected, none is collected, used or disclosed about children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions: ansarwub@gmail.com
