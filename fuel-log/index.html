Fuel & Mileage Log - Privacy Policy

Effective 9 November 2026 · Publisher: Tosa Creations Ltd

In short

Everything you enter (vehicles, fill-ups, odometer readings, costs, notes, reminders) is stored on your phone. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The app works fully offline; it uses the internet only if you send us feedback from inside the app. Data leaves the phone only in ways you control: Android's own backup to your Google account (if it is on in your phone settings; it includes only one file, your fuel log, not the app's other storage), files you export or share, and feedback you choose to send.

Data we collect

None, unless you choose to send us feedback. We never receive, store or share your fuel log. The only permission the app asks you for is Notifications, and only when you add a service reminder with a date, so the phone can remind you that morning. You can say no or turn it off at any time; the reminder still shows inside the app. The app does not use exact alarms. Android also lists two automatic permissions that come with reminders (start after the phone restarts, keep the phone awake for a moment) so a planned reminder is not lost after a restart. Two more appear in the app's details: Google Play Billing (only if you buy Pro, handled by Google Play) and internet (only to send a feedback message you choose to write).

Feedback (only if you choose)

The Feedback button opens a small box inside the app. If you write a message and tap Send, we receive your message, the app version, the language, your Android version and phone model, and the screen you were on. A phone number or e-mail address is sent only if you type one (it is optional, for a reply). No account, ID, location or fuel data is sent. Without internet the message waits on your phone and is sent the next time you are online. The messages are stored on our own server (Cloudflare, Tosa Creations account) only to reply to you and improve the app; we do not share or sell them. Each message is deleted automatically 12 months after it arrives. To limit spam, the server counts messages per network per day using a one-way code that changes daily; your IP address is not stored. To have a message deleted sooner, write to support@tosacreations.com. You can also choose "Send by e-mail instead", which opens your own e-mail app with a draft, and nothing is sent until you press Send there. The app's internet access is limited to this feedback address; it does not contact any other server.

Photo scan (only if you use it)

The "Scan pump / receipt" and "Scan odometer" buttons open your phone's own camera app or photo picker. The photo is read on your phone by a text-recognition model that is built into the app (Google ML Kit, on-device). The photo is not uploaded, not kept by the app after it is read, and the app needs no camera permission. Only the numbers you then save are stored, like a normal entry.

Your files

CSV export and full backup create a file only when you tap the button, and you choose where it goes (the system share sheet or file picker). Import and restore read only the file you pick. Your CSV and backup files are yours; keep them safe, because anyone who has the file can read it. If Android backup is switched on in your phone settings, Android copies the app's data to your own Google account backup (encrypted by Google, never sent to us) and can restore it when you set up a new phone with the same Google account (this depends on your phone and Android settings). The app itself does not send your log anywhere; its only internet use is the optional feedback message described above. You can switch Android backup off in your phone settings, and you can also keep the app's own backup file.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes all data from the phone. If Android backup is on, a reinstall on the same Google account can bring the log back. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it for you; export a CSV or backup first if you want to keep it.

Children

The app is a general tool for drivers and is not directed to children.

Purchases

Fuel Log Pro is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the app only asks Google Play on your phone whether Pro is active and does not see or store your card or payment details.

Changes

If this ever changes (for example, if ads are added), this page will be updated before the app is.
