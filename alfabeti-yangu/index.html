Taarifa kamili kwa Kiswahili

Taarifa za kujifunza kwenye simu yako

Programu haihitaji akaunti. Haina matangazo wala huduma ya kuchanganua matumizi. Taarifa za kujifunza hubaki katika hifadhi ya programu kwenye simu yako: herufi zilizotazamwa, nyota na vibandiko, majaribio ya mazoezi na matokeo yake, tarehe za shughuli, maendeleo katika masomo, orodha ya picha za kutafuta, kundi la umri lililochaguliwa, na mipangilio ya sauti, kasi ya sauti na ukubwa wa maandishi. Nakala ya ndani husaidia kurejesha taarifa za maendeleo zilizoharibika. Hii si nakala ya mtandaoni.

Programu haiombi jina la mtoto, anwani ya barua pepe, picha, mahali alipo, orodha ya anwani, kamera au kipaza sauti. Wazazi wanaweza kufuta taarifa za kujifunza na mipangilio kwenye ukurasa wa wazazi. Kufuta hifadhi ya programu katika Android au kuondoa programu pia hufuta data yake ya ndani. Nakala za data kwenye wingu na uhamisho wa data kwenda kifaa kingine vimezimwa katika mipangilio ya programu ya Android.

Rekodi na huduma ya sauti ya simu

Masomo na rekodi zilizomo kwenye programu hufanya kazi bila intaneti. Rekodi ikikosekana au ikishindwa kucheza, programu inaweza kutoa maandishi ya somo, lugha na mipangilio ya uchezaji kwa huduma ya simu inayogeuza maandishi kuwa sauti. Programu ya Android hukagua sauti iliyosakinishwa ya lugha inayofaa ambayo simu inaripoti kuwa ya ndani. Hukataa sauti zisizopatikana au zinazohitaji mtandao. Ikiwa hakuna sauti inayofaa, huonyesha ujumbe kwamba sauti haipatikani.

Huduma ya sauti ya simu hutolewa tofauti na programu hii. Utendaji wake na namna inavyoshughulikia data hutegemea mtoa huduma huyo na mipangilio ya kifaa. Programu haitumi maendeleo ya kujifunza, mipangilio ya umri au maoni ya mzazi kwa huduma hiyo. Hatudai kudhibiti huduma ya sauti ya kila mtengenezaji wa simu.

Maoni ya hiari ya mzazi

Programu hufanya kazi kikamilifu bila intaneti. Intaneti hutumika tu mzazi anapotuma maoni. Kabla ya kutoa maoni, kuna swali la hesabu kwa mtu mzima. Kisha mzazi huandika ujumbe ndani ya programu; hatuombi jina, barua pepe wala namba ya simu. Mzazi akibonyeza "Tuma", programu hutuma kwa seva yetu ya maoni (api.tosacreations.com): ujumbe, jina na toleo la programu, lugha, aina ya simu na toleo la Android. Seva pia hurekodi tarehe na nchi. Hatuhifadhi anwani ya IP. Historia ya kujifunza ya mtoto au mipangilio yake haitumwi. Kama hakuna intaneti, ujumbe hubaki kwenye simu na hutumwa wenyewe intaneti ikirudi. Programu haiungani na anwani nyingine yoyote ya intaneti.

Kisanduku cha maoni pia kina kitufe cha "Tuma kwa barua pepe". Ukitutumia barua pepe, tunapokea anwani yako ya barua pepe na chochote unachoweka katika ujumbe kupitia mtoa huduma wetu wa barua pepe. Tunatumia mawasiliano haya kujibu maoni na kuboresha programu. Tafadhali usiweke jina la mtoto, picha au taarifa nyingine zinazomtambulisha. Unaweza kuomba kufutwa kwa mawasiliano haya kupitia support@tosacreations.com. Mtoa huduma wako wa barua pepe pia hushughulikia ujumbe kulingana na sera yake.

Privacy notice — Alfabeti Yangu

Learning on your device

The app does not require an account. It has no advertising or analytics service. Learning activity stays in the app's storage on your device: letters explored, stars and stickers, practice attempts and results, activity dates, progress through lessons, the picture-finding checklist, the selected age band, and sound, voice-speed and text-size preferences. A local recovery copy helps restore damaged progress entries. This is not an online backup.

The app does not ask for the child's name, email address, photo, location, contacts, camera or microphone. Parents can erase learning activity and preferences from the parents' screen. Clearing Android app storage or uninstalling the app also removes its local data. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled in the app's configuration.

Recordings and the phone's speech service

Bundled lessons and recordings work offline. When a recording is missing or fails to play, the app may pass the lesson text, language and playback settings to the phone's text-to-speech service. The Android app checks for a matching installed voice reported as local and rejects unavailable or network-required voices. If no suitable voice is available, it shows an unavailable message.

The phone's speech service is provided separately from this app. Its operation and any data handling are governed by that provider and the device settings. The app does not send learning progress, age settings or parent feedback to that service. We do not claim to control every phone manufacturer's speech engine.

Optional parent feedback

The app works fully offline. It uses the internet only if a parent sends feedback. Feedback is behind a grown-up arithmetic check. The parent then types a message inside the app; no name, email address or phone number is asked. When the parent taps Send, the app sends to our feedback server (api.tosacreations.com): the message, the app name and version, the language, the phone model and Android version. The server also records the date and country. We do not store IP addresses. The child's learning history and settings are not sent. Without internet, the message waits on the phone and is sent automatically when the phone is back online. The app connects to no other internet address.

The feedback box also has a "Send by email" button. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message through our email provider. We use this correspondence to respond to feedback and improve the app. Please avoid including a child's name, photo or other identifying details. You can request deletion of correspondence by contacting support@tosacreations.com. Your email provider also handles messages under its own policy.

We keep feedback messages and support emails for 12 months, then delete them. Request deletion at support@tosacreations.com.
