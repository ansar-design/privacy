In English

Publisher: Tosa Creations Limited (since 3 November 2026; earlier published on Google Play under the name "Amirah's World"). Updated 14 November 2026 (version 1.5).

All learning in Nur Qaida works offline and sends nothing to us. Exception: the optional "write it" letter tracing, on first use and only if you agree, downloads an Arabic handwriting model (about 20 MB) once from Google servers; Your handwriting, voice and progress are never sent. It has no ads and no accounts, and we use no analytics of our own. Only if you use letter tracing, Google ML Kit itself may send Google diagnostics (device and app info, performance, error codes and, per Google, possibly a device or other identifier), used by Google only for diagnostics and usage analytics and not passed to third parties. "Record yourself" uses the microphone only while you speak (max 30 s, 90 s for the whole Fatiha); the audio stays in the phone's memory and is never saved or sent. Lesson progress (cards done, tracing stars, quiz best, streak, 30-day plan start) and settings (large text, slow audio, guides heard, AI-recitation notice) are stored only on your device; Android backup is turned off for this app.

Optional "Recitation donation": only if you consent (18+) and press "Send" for each clip, the app sends that audio clip, which ayah/syllable it is, optional age group and gender, a random app id (for deletion), consent time and app version over HTTPS. Clips are received by a Google Apps Script and saved in a secure Google Drive folder, with one row per clip in a Google Sheets log, all inside Tosa Creations Limited's own Google Workspace account. Tosa Creations Limited decides how the clips are used and is responsible for them (data controller). Google acts as our storage processor under its own terms. Only the project team and the volunteer (unpaid) qari reviewers we choose for this work can open the folder; they listen to the clips and mark pronunciation mistakes. Clips are used only to build and improve a free, ad-free Quran recitation tutor for Bangla speakers. They are not sold, not used for ads, and not shared with anyone other than Google as storage processor and those reviewers. Kept for the life of the project, at most 3 years.

Deletion: the in-app button "withdraw consent and delete all my recordings" moves every clip of your app id to Google Drive trash at once, and Google permanently deletes trashed files within 30 days; the log rows (item, age group, app id, consent time; no audio) stay, marked "deleted", as a record that consent was withdrawn, and are deleted at the end of the 3-year project limit. Age is self-declared: before donating you must confirm you are 18 or older; the app does not verify it. You can also email us. If a model was already trained with a clip before you deleted it, the clip cannot be taken out of that trained model, but deleted clips are not used in any later training. With a donation we never take your name, phone, email, location, contacts, device ID or advertising ID (ML Kit diagnostics above are separate and go only to Google).

Optional feedback box: the "report a problem / mistake" and "suggest" buttons on the About page and the "⚑ pronunciation mistake?" button in each lesson open a text box. Only when you press "Send" do we receive your message, the screen or letter it came from (e.g. "Lesson 2 · بَ"), app version, language, Android version and phone model, and a phone number or e-mail only if you type one (optional, for a reply). No audio, name, location or device ID is sent. It goes over HTTPS to our own server (Cloudflare, api.tosacreations.com); to block spam (max 200 per network per day) the server keeps a daily-changing hash of the network address, never the IP itself. Offline, the message waits on the phone and is sent when the internet returns. Messages are used only to fix the app and reply to you, never sold or shared, and kept at most 12 months, then deleted automatically; email md@tosacreations.com to delete.
