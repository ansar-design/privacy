Amirah's Alphabet — Privacy Policy

Effective 19 October 2026 · App ID com.ansarullah.amirahalphabet

Amirah's Alphabet is a learning app for young children. It teaches Bangla and English letters and numbers with pictures, sound and finger tracing. It is made by Syed Ansar Ullah, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not ask for a name, email, age, photo, location or any other personal information, and it has no sign-up or login.

What stays on the device

The number of stars a child has earned and the chosen voice settings are saved only on the device itself, so the app remembers them next time. This never leaves the device, and deleting the app removes it.

No ads, no tracking, no sharing

The app shows no advertising, contains no analytics or tracking tools, has no in-app purchases, has no links to social media, and shares no data with anyone.

Sound

Letters are read aloud using recorded audio inside the app or the device's own text-to-speech engine. The app does not use the microphone or camera.

Children

The app is designed for children aged 3 to 6 with a parent nearby. Because no personal information is collected, no information from children is collected, used or disclosed.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: support@tosacreations.com
