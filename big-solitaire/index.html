Privacy policy — Big Print Solitaire & Mahjong

App ID: com.tosacreations.bigsolitaire · Publisher: Tosa Creations Limited · Contact: support@tosacreations.com · Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short

You can play without an account and without internet. The game does not ask for your name, email, contacts, precise location or photos; only if you send feedback do we receive what you type. Online ads and purchases involve the services described below. There are no full-screen ads; a small banner may appear below the game buttons.

What stays on your phone

Your games in progress, statistics and settings are saved on your device; we do not send them to a game server. Android backup or device transfer may copy or restore app data according to your device settings. Clearing app storage removes the local game data. Google Play keeps your purchase history separately.

Ads (Google AdMob)

If you have not removed ads, after your first 3 finished games the app shows one small banner ad from Google AdMob in a strip below the game buttons. It never covers the cards or tiles and there are no full-screen ads. To choose and show ads, Google collects and shares IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID, app/ad interactions, and performance diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, as described in Google's advertising policy. The app checks Google's consent requirements before requesting ads and shows a consent form when required and available. If eligibility cannot be confirmed, it does not request ads. Open Settings → Remove ads & privacy → Ad privacy choices to review choices when Google makes them available. Opening a required privacy form removes the banner for the session; reopening the app checks eligibility again. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Android settings.

Removing ads (purchases)

You can remove ads with a monthly or yearly plan or a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google Play handles payment; we do not receive your payment card details. To validate purchases, the app sends receipt data, including product/package identifiers, a purchase token and purchase/order information, over HTTPS to tosacreations.com/api/billing/v1/validate. Our validator processes it with Google Play to check entitlement, expiry and refunds. Its code does not store receipts in a database or log purchase tokens. Our hosting provider processes network requests to operate this service.

Feedback (optional)

If you choose to use Settings → Report a problem or Suggest an idea, the app sends your message, the app version, the screen you were on, your phone model and Android version, and an email or phone number only if you type one, over HTTPS to api.tosacreations.com/feedback, hosted by our provider Cloudflare. We use it only to fix problems, improve the app and reply to you; we do not sell it or use it for ads. If you are offline, the message waits on your phone and is sent later. You can instead send it by email. Ask us at the address below to delete a message.

Children

The app is made for adults (18+) and is not directed at children.

Other services and deletion

We do not add a separate game analytics service or sell your game saves. Google advertising and purchase services process data under their own policies. Resetting an advertising ID does not erase all information already held by Google; see Google’s privacy policy for its data controls and retention practices. Contact us about data requests at the address below. Links to external policies need internet; this page is available offline in the app.

Contact and changes

Questions or deletion requests: support@tosacreations.com. If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
